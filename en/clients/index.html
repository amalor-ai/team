The familiar problem

Why software projects so often cost too much and disappoint

When you order software from a development firm, you pay for the whole machine: sales, project management, offices and margin. The people who build your software work for their employer – their salary is the same whether your solution succeeds or not.

with a service provider

Classic contract

  • Your budget funds the provider’s overhead.
  • The developers work for their employer, not for your result.
  • Billing by the hour: more effort means more revenue.
  • After the project the team moves on to the next client.

built by a team that stays

With us

  • 80% of your budget goes to the people who build your solution.
  • You only pay for what you have accepted.
  • If someone drops out, AMALOR provides a replacement.
  • As a partner, you and the team become co-founders of the solution.

Ownership is the strongest motivation. Whoever holds a share in the solution wants it to really work – not just to get the project invoiced.

The vision

First the best software for your company. Then for your industry.

Companies in one industry compete in their business, not in their software. That is how DATEV began: German tax advisers built their software together and still own it as a cooperative. Whoever builds first sets the standard. Each step builds on the one before, none is required.

  1. Diagnostictwo weeks, fixed price
  2. Your own softwarebuilt for your company, proven in real use
  3. Startupyou and the team found it together – under its own name
  4. Industry customersother companies use the solution, data strictly separated
  5. Cooperativethe industry becomes a co-owner of the software

When the industry joins

Companies in the industrybuy shares in the cooperative: a vote and lower licence prices
The cooperativebecomes the majority owner of the startup – the software belongs to the industry, not to a corporation
You as the founderkeep founder shares, free use and a seat on the supervisory board
The teamkeeps its shares and keeps developing the software

Whoever thinks of everyone gets the best. The first client sets the standard of its industry – and gets the solution built exactly for it.

Frequently asked questions

What kind of tasks do you solve?

Problems of small and mid-sized companies that cost time and money: processes, automation, customer handling, data, launching new lines of business. We take on tasks that pass our filter: people need them and are willing to pay.

Where do you work?

Remotely, wherever you are. We work in English, Russian and other languages — choose yours in the form.

How does it start and what does it cost?

With a free 30-minute conversation. Then a two-week diagnostic at a fixed price: you receive an analysis of the problem and a solution plan with price and timeline. After that, work proceeds in stages, each priced up front.

Who is accountable for the result?

The whole team. The Team Lead is your single contact, but every member stands behind the result.

What if a stage is not right?

You accept every stage. If a stage is not accepted, the team finishes it at its own cost.

Can I take part if I have a main job?

Yes. We match your role to the time you can give, from a couple of hours a week to full-time.
